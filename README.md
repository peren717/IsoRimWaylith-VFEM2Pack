# IsoRim Waylith - VFE Medieval 2 Pack

3D models for [Vanilla Factions Expanded - Medieval 2](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3444347874) in the 3D view of [IsoRim Waylith](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-1.7) (RimWorld 1.7).

- Castle walls with battlements, gates and wall-mounted turrets.
- Armour, helmets, shields and tabards.
- Medieval and Viking furniture, workbenches and walls.

Requires IsoRim Waylith and Vanilla Factions Expanded - Medieval 2. Also supports [IsoRimZ](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-ProjectZ) (optional, multiple floors). Load after IsoRim Waylith and VFE Medieval 2.

Vanilla Factions Expanded - Medieval 2 is made by Oskar Potocki and the Vanilla Expanded team. This pack only adds 3D models and contains no files of the original mod.
